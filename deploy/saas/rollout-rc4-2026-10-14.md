## Deliberate implementation choices

- Preserve the original free deadline using a billing-cycle anchor and no
  proration. Checkout trial_end has a minimum duration that excludes the last
  48hours. Persist exact parameters for retries; definitive stale-anchor errors
  prompt a new attempt; ambiguous errors retain idempotency.
- Keep WAL synchronous FULL for credential and billing durability. NORMAL's
  power-loss tradeoff is inappropriate for acknowledged secret rotation.
- Index every owned record for complete deletion/revocation; account views
  decrypt only relevant grants/installations belonging to that user.
