Your AI agent.

Give your terminal-enabled AI agent concrete tools to inspect accounts, draft posts and prepare approved actions on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. SocialCli connects your conversation to a reviewable workflow.

Install SocialCliWork with your AI agent →
socialcli brand new MyBrand
socialcli publish my-video --brand MyBrand --only tiktok --dry-run

Let your agent prepare

Ask your assistant to check connections, draft captions and show the complete preview. Approve the exact submission before it acts.

Keep brands separate

Each brand keeps its accounts, credentials, posts and records in its own folder.

Stay in control

Use files you have already produced. SocialCli does not generate or crop media and distinguishes pending submissions from published posts.

Start with a conversation

“Check MyBrand’s TikTok connection without publishing.”

“Prepare my video’s caption and show the complete dry-run preview. Wait for my approval.”

Available to other creators

Download the code, install the CLI and connect your own accounts using provider applications you configure. No other creator's credentials are required or included. This distribution does not yet offer a shared SocialCli OAuth backend.

Understand the TikTok flow

The default mode is inbox: SocialCli sends your video to the inbox and you finish publishing in TikTok. Production APIs and Direct Post require provider authorization and review.

The public repository does not establish TikTok production approval. Direct Post UX controls remain incomplete. Check each integration's status and requirements before using it.
