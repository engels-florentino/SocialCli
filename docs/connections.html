Connect your accounts

Authorize your own eligible social accounts in the browser. Shared mode does not require your own provider Developer app. Each platform must first be configured by the service operator and receive the required permissions; the current pilot is not announced as publicly approved.

socialcli connect tiktok --brand MyBrand
socialcli connect youtube --brand MyBrand
socialcli connect facebook --brand MyBrand
socialcli connect instagram --brand MyBrand
socialcli connections --brand MyBrand --json
socialcli disconnect youtube --brand MyBrand

Hosted access today

Anyone can install the public CLI. Hosted TikTok access is currently sandbox-only for authorized target accounts. Google is in Testing for authorized test users; Meta is unpublished with development access. Installation does not make your account eligible. If your account cannot authorize, wait for production availability or use a service whose operator has the required provider access.

What you do

Run connect, sign in on the provider’s own page, approve permissions, then return to SocialCli and confirm the displayed channel or account. For Facebook choose a Page you administer. Instagram requires a professional account linked to that Page in this integration. TikTok uses inbox upload; finish posting in TikTok.

Optional analytics access

socialcli connect youtube --brand MyBrand --analytics
socialcli connect facebook --brand MyBrand --analytics
socialcli connect instagram --brand MyBrand --analytics

Facebook adds read_insights; Instagram with Facebook Login adds instagram_manage_insights. Disconnect an existing shared connection before reconnecting with additional permissions. TikTok analytics is not supported by this connection flow.

Optional Meta management access

socialcli connect facebook --brand MyBrand --management --analytics
socialcli connect instagram --brand MyBrand --management --analytics

Facebook management adds pages_read_user_content, pages_manage_engagement and pages_manage_metadata. Instagram management adds instagram_manage_comments and pages_manage_metadata. Analytics remains optional. Update the client and reconnect existing accounts to grant additional permissions. Declined required permissions reject the connection. Consent does not subscribe webhooks or approve any comment, moderation or publication action. External creators require the corresponding Meta Advanced Access and review.

What stays protected

Our connection service keeps provider app secrets and encrypted refresh tokens. Your OS keyring stores the connection capability. Access tokens remain only in the CLI process. Creator media stays on your computer or your own hosting.

Renewal and disconnect

Google and TikTok tokens can renew server-side during authorized operations. Meta and revoked or expired permissions may require reconnecting. Disconnect removes the service connection; revoke SocialCli in provider settings to also withdraw provider authorization.

AI agents

Agents can inspect connection status and start the flow. The creator completes login and confirms the account. Connecting never approves a post: require the complete dry-run preview and explicit approval for every submission.

Availability and setup

The service runs at social.florentino.pro. Disabled connectors fail clearly. The existing independent-app auth mode remains available. Read the complete connection guide and self-hosting instructions.
