Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026. SocialCli is operated by Engels Florentino at socialcli.app and its pilot subdomain. Contact: blades3@gmail.com.

Your account and sign-in

We use your email address to send one-time sign-in codes and service messages. Our self-hosted Supabase Auth service handles identity verification. Our SMTP service delivers codes, trial reminders and operational notices; email delivery providers may process recipients, message content and delivery metadata. Browser sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies and encrypted access and refresh tokens. Sessions last at most 12 hours; identity checks may be cached for up to 45 seconds. Cookies are used for sign-in, CSRF protection and account connection, not advertising.

Social accounts and assistants

We process the brand label, account identifiers and names, granted permissions, authorization codes, provider tokens and assistant installation credentials needed for your requested operations. Credentials are encrypted at rest with authenticated encryption. Your chosen assistant receives scoped SocialCli connection capabilities and may retrieve provider access tokens. Provider permissions can cover more than a single action. Your passwords stay on provider sign-in pages. Connecting, paying or linking an assistant does not approve a publication.

Local creator workspaces, content, metrics and operation records remain on the computer or runner you choose. Your assistant provider handles information you share with it under its own policies. SocialCli does not contain a built-in AI model, sell account data, use it for advertising or use it to train models.

Temporary Instagram files

When your SaaS Instagram connection needs a public media URL, SocialCli uploads your supplied JPEG, MP4 or MOV file to temporary storage on our florentino.pro server. Instagram retrieves it using an unguessable HTTPS link. Anyone with that link may retrieve the file while it is valid, so do not share it. The link expires 15 minutes after reservation, including upload time. The client requests deletion when its operation ends; automatic cleanup removes expired and interrupted files. Cleanup also runs on service startup and through an independent server timer. During an infrastructure outage, physical deletion resumes when the server runs again. Files are excluded from application backups. This service is not permanent storage: keep your originals. Files published on a social platform follow that platform’s retention policy.

Subscriptions and payment records

Stripe handles Checkout, payment methods, invoices and the billing portal under Stripe’s privacy policy. We store customer, subscription and invoice-event identifiers, payment status and access dates to provide and reconcile your USD 10 monthly subscription. We do not receive full card details. During the pilot, billing uses Stripe test mode.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while needed to provide your account. Sign-in and social authorization attempts expire after ten minutes; assistant pairing follows the expiry shown on its link. Browser sessions expire within 12 hours. Social connection inactivity and assistant credential expiry are enforced by the service. Expired records and rate-limit counters are periodically pruned. Rate keys use hashes rather than raw IP addresses or emails.

Disconnect removes the relevant service credentials. Revoke an assistant or request account deletion at Account. Deletion immediately blocks local service access and queues cancellation of billing and removal of the identity account, retrying if a provider is unavailable. It does not delete published posts. Previously issued provider tokens may remain valid until provider expiry or revocation; remove SocialCli from provider settings to revoke its grant.

To prevent repeated free trials, we retain a keyed HMAC eligibility marker derived from your normalized email, with the original trial dates, for 365 days from trial activation, including after account deletion. This marker is pseudonymous, not anonymous; it contains no plain-text email. Renaming your brand or reconnecting does not reset trial eligibility.

Full signed billing webhook payloads are kept for at most seven days and removed earlier after processing; minimal event hashes, IDs and reconciliation status are retained for audit and duplicate detection. Necessary payment records remain with Stripe under its retention rules. Protected encrypted account backups may contain earlier account data until the operator removes those backups; contact us for a deletion or retention request. temporary media is not included. Logs exclude credentials and are size-limited. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Other services and your choices

Authorized requests send required data to Google/YouTube, Meta/Facebook/Instagram or TikTok. Independent application credentials and self-hosted deployments follow the storage choices and operator policies you select. Public source and issues are hosted by GitHub; never post private data or credentials there. The public site has no advertising or first-party analytics. Hosting infrastructure processes technical requests needed to serve the site.

Contact

To request access, correction or deletion, or ask about retention, email blades3@gmail.com. Do not include tokens, passwords or keys. We may verify ownership before acting on an account request. We will update the effective date when this policy changes.
