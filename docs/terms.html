Plan, trial and cancellation

The plan costs USD 10 per month for one brand and one connected account per supported platform. Your 14-day trial begins with your first successful social connection; no card is required to start. You may subscribe during the trial without losing remaining days. The first monthly charge is due at the original trial end, then monthly. If you subscribe after it ends, the first charge is due when you confirm Checkout. Any applicable tax is shown before payment confirmation. During the pilot, Stripe test mode does not collect live payments.

Paid subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Manage payment details and cancel through Account and the Stripe billing portal. The portal shows the effective cancellation and access dates. Trial access ends without payment if you do not subscribe. Account deletion requests cancellation of future billing. Reconnecting or renaming a brand does not grant a new trial. Temporary media

Temporary Instagram hosting accepts supplied originals up to 512 MiB, subject to account and server quotas. Links expire 15 minutes after reservation, including upload time, and files are automatically removed as described in the privacy policy. Keep your own originals; this is not archival storage. We do not generate, edit or transcode files. Slow uploads or provider processing may fail within this time limit and require a new, explicitly reviewed attempt.
